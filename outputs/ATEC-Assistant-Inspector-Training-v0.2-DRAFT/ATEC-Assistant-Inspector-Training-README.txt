APPROVED WORKFLOW
Inspector adds the assistant to Job Crew before submission.
Inspector records the factual job timeline.
ATEC copies valid timeline segments to included crew when the job first becomes SUBMITTED.
Assistant reviews and corrects their own unlocked My Day entries.
Assistant selects Confirm and submit to manager.
Manager/Admin/HR reviews, returns or approves according to the authorised process.

EXCEPTION WORKFLOW
If the assistant was omitted and no copied time exists, the assistant uses
Add an exception or non-job activity in My Day. Job-related activities require
the numeric Accelo Job Number. The assistant must check for duplicates first.

ROLE BOUNDARY
An inspector can create crew time through the shared job timeline. The inspector
cannot submit or approve the assistant's daily timesheet. The assistant submits
their own record and management performs the review.
