SCENE 5: Why the timing matters
Screen action: Why the timing matters. ATEC copies the timeline when the job first moves into SUBMITTED. Valid segments copy to all crew members included in time. Adding an assistant after submission is not confirmed to backfill automatically. If somebody was omitted, use the controlled recovery procedure.
