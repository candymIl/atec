SCENE 2: Open ATEC and sign in
Screen action: Open ATEC and sign in. Open Microsoft Edge or the approved company browser. Use the official ATEC address or approved company shortcut. Enter your own assigned username and password. Select Login once and confirm the displayed identity is yours. Warning: Never share credentials or use another employee’s account.

SCENE 3: Open My Day
Screen action: Open My Day. From the menu, open Jobs & Time if it is grouped. Select My Day. Assistants do not use Technician Job Cards or Timesheet Approvals. Select the date on which the work was performed. Read the timesheet status before changing anything.

SCENE 4: Review every copied line
Screen action: Review every copied line. Check Activity, From, To, Customer / Job, Normal and Overtime. Look for omissions, overlaps, duplicates and the wrong job number.
Narration: Review every copied line. Check Activity, From, To, Customer / Job, Normal and Overtime. Look for omissions, overlaps, duplicates and the wrong job number.

SCENE 5: Correct and submit
Screen action: Correct and submit. If an unlocked line is wrong, select Edit or Delete. Enter correct start/end values and a clear audit reason of at least five characters. Confirm the day has no missing or duplicate periods. Select Confirm and submit to manager. Open the FBC009-10 PDF if required and verify the record. Warning: A submitted timesheet is read-only. Ask the manager to return it if correction is required.
